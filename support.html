How does a flight work?

Pip launches from a sky island and glides as far as possible. Pitch down to gain speed, pull up to trade it for height; fly too slowly and Pip stalls. Ride thermals for free height, fly along wind ribbons for speed and skim low over island tops to build a combo. The flight ends when Pip sinks into the cloud sea.

What are beacons?

Beacons stand along the archipelago. Fly past one to light it: it pays feathers the first time and becomes a launch point on the sky map, so later flights can start further out. Twelve beacons lead through four skies to the Star Nest.

Pip keeps sinking too early

Spend feathers on glider upgrades: better wings sink slower, thermal sense makes thermals stronger. Save a flap for when you're low. If it's still too hard, try Settings → Gameplay → Difficulty assist.

Up and down feel the wrong way round

Turn on invert up/down in Settings → Gameplay.

The FLAP button is too small or too big

Change it in Settings → Touch controls → FLAP button size.

The game runs slowly or my phone gets warm

Set Settings → Gameplay → Graphics to Low.

What are boosters?

Boosters are one-flight helpers: High Launch (60 feathers), Extra Flaps (80) and Skim Double (100). Buy them with feathers in the shop, or win them from some missions. You can take up to 2 on a flight; Retry reuses them if you still have some. Feathers can't be bought with money.

What is the Daily Wind?

Once the first beacon is lit, a new world opens every day, the same for every player. Your best distance of the day is kept.

Who is Wren?

Wren is a swallow who joins Pip once three beacons are lit. Wren flies beside Pip and finds 10% extra feathers on every flight (shown on the results screen).

How do I turn off the characters' speech bubbles?

Turn off Settings → Gameplay → Character chat. The guides never pause the game while they talk. You can read about every friend you have met in the Wardrobe.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game works offline. Only cloud saves and ads need a connection.

Progress & cloud saves

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Games on Android. The game keeps a copy of your progress in that account and restores it on any device signed in to the same account. You can check the status in Settings → Saved progress.

Without it, progress stays on the device.

My progress didn't come back after reinstalling

Make sure you're signed in to the same Game Center or Google Play Games account as before, then open Settings → Saved progress and wait a moment while it syncs. If it still doesn't appear, email support.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Settings → Saved progress → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This can't be undone.

Ads & privacy

When do ads appear?

Only when you choose to watch one: to lift Pip back out of the cloud sea once, or to double the feathers from a flight. You can always just fly again instead. The desktop version has no ads.

No ad is offered when Pip sinks

You may be offline, or no ad is available right now. Try again later, or just fly again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings → About → Privacy choices (ads). On iPhone and iPad, tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What data does the game collect?

Pip's Glide has no accounts and the developer collects no personal information. Google collects some data to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.

Problems & bugs

I found a bug

Email support with what happened, how far Pip had flown and, if you can, a screenshot or screen recording. It helps a lot.

There's no sound

Check the volumes in Settings → Sound, and that your device's volume is turned up. If it still doesn't play, email support.
